SEO Facet

Privacy Policy
In short: SEO Facet runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data, and there is no server behind it.

Effective date: 12 August 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the SEO Facet browser extension ("SEO Facet", "the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use it. We are committed to protecting your privacy. SEO Facet is designed to operate entirely within your browser and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to the SEO Facet extension for browsers built on Chromium, including Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. It does not apply to any website you analyze with the Extension, each of which is governed by its own privacy practices.

3. Information We Process

When you request an analysis, SEO Facet reads the content of the web page currently open in your active browser tab. This may include the page title, meta tags, heading structure, images, links, and structured data. This processing occurs locally on your device, solely to display SEO information to you, and only at your request. The Extension does not create a user account and does not require you to provide any personal information.

4. Local Processing

All analysis performed by SEO Facet takes place within your browser. We do not operate any server, database, or backend infrastructure that receives your data. The results of an analysis remain on your device and are not transmitted to us or to any external party.

5. Network Requests Initiated by You

Certain optional features require requests to external websites. These features run only when you explicitly activate them:

  • Broken Link Check submits requests to the links found on the current page in order to report their response status.
  • Technical Audit requests the current page along with its robots.txt file and sitemap in order to assess crawlability and indexation.

In each case, the requests are sent directly from your browser to the relevant websites. They do not pass through any infrastructure operated by us, and we do not receive, log, or retain them. The websites you contact in this manner process your request in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Permissions

SEO Facet requests the minimum browser permissions necessary to function:

  • activeTab and scripting: to read the content of the current page when you request an analysis.
  • sidePanel: to display results within the browser side panel.
  • offscreen: to detect whether your browser is set to light or dark mode so that the toolbar icon can match it.
  • Host access (declared as <all_urls>): to allow the Extension to operate on any website you choose to analyze and to permit the optional checks described above to reach the relevant pages, robots.txt files, and sitemaps.

7. Data Retention and Storage

SEO Facet does not maintain a history of the pages you view and stores no personal data on any remote system. Limited interface preferences, if any, are stored locally within your browser and may be removed by uninstalling the Extension or clearing your browser data.

8. Disclosure of Information

Because SEO Facet does not collect personal data, we do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose personal information to any third party. We may disclose information only where required to do so by applicable law, although in practice we hold no personal data that could be subject to such a request.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under laws such as the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. Because SEO Facet does not collect or store personal data, we hold no such information to provide, amend, or erase. If you have questions about these rights, you may contact us using the details below.

10. International Users

SEO Facet processes information locally within your browser regardless of your location. As no data is transmitted to or stored by us, no international transfer of personal data occurs through your use of the Extension.

11. Security

Because SEO Facet does not collect or transmit your data, the associated privacy risk is minimal by design. All processing remains on your device and under your control.

12. Children's Privacy

SEO Facet is a professional tool intended for website owners, developers, and marketers. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be reflected by revising the effective date shown above. Continued use of the Extension after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

14. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at seofacet@gmail.com.
